# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and inclusive. Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people. Respect customer confidentiality, intellectual property, and the boundary between public product information and internal material.

Examples of welcome behavior include useful feedback, patient explanation, responsible disclosure, and disagreement supported by evidence. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of confidential information, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report security-sensitive concerns through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Report other conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
